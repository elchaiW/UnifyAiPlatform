PRIVACY POLICY FOR TECH STARTUP

DATA COLLECTION:
We collect the following types of information:
- Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number)
- Usage data and analytics
- Technical information about your device and browser

DATA USAGE:
Your data is used for the following purposes:
- Providing and improving our services
- Communicating with you about updates and features
- Analyzing usage patterns to enhance user experience

LEGAL BASIS:
Under GDPR, we process your data based on:
- Consent for marketing communications
- Legitimate interest for service provision

RETENTION POLICY:
We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Personal data is deleted within 30 days of account closure unless required by law.

YOUR RIGHTS:
Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to data processing
- Data portability
